Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Daily Bhajan Lives respects your privacy. This starter policy describes how the website may handle information. Update it to reflect the actual services, analytics, hosting, and integrations used before publishing.

Information

The static website does not intentionally ask visitors to submit personal information. If contact forms, accounts, analytics, embedded media, or an AI assistant are added, those services may process information such as messages, device data, or usage details.

Third-party services

Links and remote assets may be provided by third parties, including Facebook, Google Fonts, Tailwind CDN, and image hosts. Their own privacy policies govern their services.

AI assistant

If an AI feature is connected, explain what messages are sent to the provider, how they are retained, and how visitors can request deletion. Never expose private API keys in browser code.

Contact and updates

Add a monitored contact email and the responsible site operator's details before launch. This policy may be updated when the website's features change.

This is a general template, not legal advice. Have the final policy reviewed for your location and actual data practices.
